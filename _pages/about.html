Junyu Chen, Ph.D.

Instructor, Russell H. Morgan Department of Radiology and Radiological Science
Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine

Curriculum Vitae Google Scholar GitHub Email

I am an Instructor in the Radiological Physics Division of the Russell H. Morgan Department of Radiology and Radiological Science at Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine. My research develops machine learning methods for medical image analysis, with a focus on image registration, segmentation, and denoising across nuclear medicine (PET and SPECT), CT, and MRI.

I work closely with Dr. Yong Du in the Quantitative Imaging Biomarker (QIB) Lab, and with Drs. Aaron Carass and Jerry Prince in the Image Analysis and Communications Lab (IACL) in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. I also maintain an informal affiliation with the KU Lab led by Dr. Katsuyuki “Ken” Taguchi.

I am an active member of the MICCAI Society, where I serve on the committee of the Special Interest Group in Biomedical Image Registration (SIG-BIR) and co-organized the Learn2Reg 2024 and 2025 image registration challenges. Within the Society of Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging (SNMMI), I serve as a non-voting member of the Board of Directors of the Physics, Instrumentation & Data Sciences Council (PIDSC) for a two-year term that began in July 2025.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Johns Hopkins University in 2022, advised by Dr. Eric C. Frey, following an M.S.E. from Johns Hopkins (2019) and dual B.Sc. degrees, summa cum laude, in Computer Engineering and Electrical Engineering from North Carolina State University (2017).
